> **"Your 24/7 Personal Exam Mentor & Cognitive Study Partner."**

## 🌟 1. Overview & Persona

**Minii** is the native intelligence engine of **MockS**. Unlike standard generic chatbots, Minii is an **Algorithmic Cognitive Agent** specifically designed for competitive exams (JEE, NEET, UPSC, SSC, Boards) and coaching workflows.

### 🎭 Brand Personality & Tone:
* **Friendly & Peer-Driven:** Warm, encouraging, and approachable — never intimidating or condescending.
* **Analytical & Sharp:** Deeply knowledgeable in science, mathematics, general studies, and exam patterns.
* **Disciplined Habit Builder:** Motivates students to replace passive social media doomscrolling with daily study streaks and targeted practice.

## ⚡ 3. Detailed Feature Breakdown

### 🎯 A. 24/7 AI Doubt Resolution Engine
* **Multimodal Question Ingestion:** Students can type doubts, paste questions, or upload handwritten notes and diagrams.
* **Socratic Step-by-Step Breakdown:** Minii breaks down complex multi-step problems (e.g., Organic Reaction Mechanisms, Rotational Mechanics, Complex Integrals) into digestible steps rather than just giving a raw answer.
* **Alternative Shortcuts & Hacks:** Offers time-saving elimination tricks and exam-tested shortcuts.

---

### ⏱️ B. Real-Time Behavioral Analysis During Tests
Minii observes how a student interacts with the CBT test engine:
* **Time-Per-Question Tracking:** Flags whether a student rushed on tricky traps or over-invested time on lengthy calculations.
* **Guess vs Knowledge Detection:** Analyzes option-switching behaviors and speed to distinguish between confident answers and pure guesses.
* **Fatigue & Focus Curve:** Measures if accuracy drops in the final 30 minutes of a 3-hour test, helping students build mental stamina.

---

### 📄 C. Automated PDF-to-CBT Test Paper Generator (For Teachers & Admins)
* **Instant Digitization:** Teachers and coaching owners can upload raw PDFs or scanned question sheets.
* **Auto-Extraction:** Minii parses:
  1. Question text & diagrams.
  2. Multiple-choice options (A, B, C, D) or Numerical values.
  3. Correct answer keys & detailed solutions.
  4. Subject, Chapter, and Difficulty tags.
* **Instant Lobby Launch:** Converts the PDF into an interactive, timed mock test ready to be scheduled for the batch in minutes.

## 🏆 5. Gamification, Streaks & Behavioral Transformation

Minii acts as a daily accountability buddy:
* **Daily Goal Setting:** Prompts students with their daily target test sets.
* **Study Streaks & XP Leaderboard:** Rewards consistency over raw brilliance to build steady study discipline.
* **Encouragement Nudges:** Sends positive reinforcement when students improve their speed or master previously difficult concepts.
