## 🎯 2. Core Mission & Value Proposition

### 🎓 For Students (Enrolled & Independent)
* **Zero Fragmentation:** No need to juggle dozens of books, websites, or separate messaging apps (WhatsApp, Telegram).
* **Vast Question Bank:** Exhaustive repository of exam-grade questions, curated tips, tricks, and time-saving strategies.
* **Exam Reality Simulation:** High-precision CBT interface calibrated so closely to official testing bodies (NTA, UPSC, SSC, Boards) that tests feel like actual testing agencies created them directly on MockS.
* **Minii AI Companion:** An omnipresent, non-judgmental algorithmic mentor guiding every step of preparation.

### 🏫 For Coaching Owners, Schools & Colleges
* **Unified Management:** One-stop administration for student rosters, batch creation, attendance, and digital fee collection.
* **Lobby Test Engine:** Ability to conduct synchronized, high-stakes mock tests for an entire batch with anti-cheat screen locks.
* **Live Performance Intelligence:** Real-time visibility into student scores, accuracy, and preparation weaknesses to provide timely encouragement.

### Key Minii Capabilities:
1. **Behavioral Learning Analysis:** Tracks hesitation times, skipped question trends, and accuracy variations across topics.
2. **24/7 AI Doubt Resolution:** Instant conceptual breakdowns with LaTeX math and diagram explanations.
3. **Automated PDF-to-Test Generator:** Empowers teachers to upload raw question PDFs and automatically transforms them into interactive CBT mock tests.
4. **Predictive Analytics:** Computes estimated All India Rank (AIR), accuracy percentiles, and subject-level mastery meters.

## 🏛️ 4. Multi-Institute Architecture & Target Verticals

MockS is strictly designed as a **Multi-Tenant, Multi-Institute Platform** where thousands of institutions operate independently while co-existing on a shared high-performance infrastructure.

### 🏷️ Institute Categorization Matrix:
* **Unique Institute IDs:** Every coaching, school, and college receives a dedicated tenant identifier.
* **Sub-Categories & Verticals:**
  * **Competitive Coaching:** JEE, NEET, UPSC, SSC, Banking, State Govt Exams.
  * **School Education:** CBSE, ICSE, State Boards (9th–12th Foundation).
  * **Colleges & Universities:** Degree-specific courses, semester exams, campus placement tests.

---

## 🤝 5. Social, Community & Peer Ecosystem

* **Institute Community Feed:** Broadcast official announcements, schedules, and verified study material directly from faculty.
* **Classmate Peer Chat:** Real-time encrypted messaging between verified batchmates to discuss problems and clarify doubts without external distractions.
* **Local-First & Offline Resilience:** SQLite local persistence allows students with low connectivity to access chats, view downloaded PDFs, and take cached practice tests completely offline.
* **Multi-Coaching Knowledge League (Future):** Cross-institute peer learning where top students from different coachings can compete, teach each other, and exchange academic strategies.

## 🛡️ 7. Anti-Cheating & Exam Integrity
For high-stakes mock exams, MockS activates a strict proctoring layer:
* 🔒 **Full Screen Lockdown:** Exiting the app or opening background tasks triggers warnings and auto-submission.
* 🚫 **Screenshot & Screen Recording Prevention:** DRM flags preventing question leaks.
* ⏱️ **Synchronized Lobby Timers:** Server-authoritative start and submission clocks.
